# n8n automation workflows

Workflows that connect AI voice agents with the CRM and team messaging.

## What they do

- Outbound dialer takes a contact list, starts AI calls and writes the results back.
- Call results become leads in the CRM, and the team gets a Telegram message.
- A callback form on the website turns into an AI phone call.
- Lead intake for BrokerDesk with a sales pipeline in HubSpot.

## Stack

n8n, Voximplant, VAPI, amoCRM API, HubSpot API, Telegram Bot API, Google Sheets API.
